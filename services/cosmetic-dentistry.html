Cosmetic Procedures

Enhance your smile with cosmetic dentistry including teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding. Achieve a confident, natural-looking smile you’ll love to share.

Request an Appointment

Teeth Whitening

Problem

Stained, dull teeth.

Solution

Customized trays are filled with whitening gel and worn for as little as one hour per day. In as little as one week, this process can erase many coffee, tobacco, and age-related stains.

Alternatives

In-office whitening offers faster results. For hard-to-treat stains, porcelain veneers or crowns may be better choices.

Bonding

Problem

Chipped, discolored, uneven, stained, or gapped teeth.

Solution

Tooth-colored composite resin is applied directly to enamel, shaped to correct the concern, and hardened with a special ultraviolet light.

Alternatives

Porcelain veneers and crowns cost more, but generally last longer than bonding.

Porcelain Veneers

Problem

Teeth that are chipped, discolored, uneven, stained, gapped, worn, crooked, or out of proportion.

Solution

Customized, wafer-thin porcelain laminates are bonded to the front surface of the teeth to create a natural-looking result that hides irregularities.

Alternatives

Orthodontic treatment or porcelain crowns may be more appropriate when decay, advanced periodontal disease, or severe misalignment is present.

Fillings

Problem

Tooth decay that has created a cavity.

Solution

After decay is removed, the tooth is restored with silver amalgam or composite resin. Composite fillings are especially popular because they blend naturally with the surrounding tooth.

Alternatives

When decay is more extensive, porcelain inlays or onlays may be recommended.

Inlays & Onlays

Problem

Tooth decay, weakened tooth structure, or a tooth that needs reinforcement.

Solution

After removing decay, an impression is taken and a laboratory creates a custom gold or porcelain inlay or onlay that fits precisely and restores strength.

Alternatives

When tooth decay is advanced, a crown may be the best alternative.

Crowns

Problem

Teeth that are chipped, cracked, badly decayed, or otherwise weakened.

Solution

Crowns cover the full visible surface of a tooth to restore appearance and provide significant reinforcement. Porcelain crowns look natural, while gold crowns can be a durable choice for back teeth.

Alternatives

When a tooth is too damaged to restore, extraction followed by a bridge or implant may be necessary.

Fixed Bridges

Problem

Missing teeth can affect chewing, speaking, facial support, and long-term bite stability.

Solution

A customized bridge uses surrounding teeth for support and restores the look and function of the missing tooth or teeth. Resin-bonded bridges may also be used in select cases.

Alternatives

Dental implants provide a stable, permanent alternative that does not rely on adjacent teeth for support.
