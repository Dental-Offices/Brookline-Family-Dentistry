Treatment Options

Safe Care When Extraction Is the Best Next Step

We focus on comfort, clear guidance, and conservative care. When a tooth cannot be preserved or wisdom teeth become problematic, we help you move through treatment with confidence.

Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth are the molars farthest back in the mouth. These teeth are the last to erupt, usually after age 18, and they often become impacted instead of growing in properly.

Impacted wisdom teeth can contribute to gum disease, infection, decay, and even cysts or tumors. To prevent those complications, wisdom teeth are often extracted at the first sign that they may be impacted.

Infection

In cases of severe infection, where restorative approaches will not save a tooth, an extraction may be necessary. We evaluate the tooth carefully and recommend removal only when it is the most reliable option for protecting your oral health.

Routine Extractions

Modern dentistry makes routine tooth extractions relatively comfortable and straightforward. We use current techniques and local anesthesia to keep the procedure as gentle as possible.

For about seven days after an extraction, the socket needs some extra care. We will walk you through aftercare, including how to brush around the area, what to expect with bleeding, and whether pain medication is appropriate for residual discomfort.
