Restorative Dentistry

Restore strength and function to damaged or missing teeth with crowns, bridges, and fillings. Our restorative dentistry ensures comfort and lasting results.

Request an Appointment

Fillings

Tooth decay can weaken enamel and lead to sensitivity or pain. Tooth-colored composite fillings restore strength and appearance while blending naturally with your smile.

Root Canal Therapy

When decay or infection reaches the inner pulp of a tooth, root canal therapy can save it from extraction. The infected tissue is removed, the canal is cleaned, and the tooth is sealed to prevent reinfection.

With modern anesthesia and technique, most patients describe the process as much easier than they expected.

Dental Crowns

If a tooth is cracked, worn down, or weakened by decay, a custom crown can restore full strength and function. Crowns can be made from porcelain, ceramic, or metal alloys and are shaped to match your bite.

Dental Bridges

A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth for support. It restores chewing function, speech, and smile balance while helping prevent shifting.

Implants

Dental implants use a small titanium post as a root replacement and a custom crown above the gumline to recreate the look and feel of a natural tooth. They also help preserve bone and protect nearby teeth.

Dentures (Full & Partial)

If you have lost several or all of your teeth, custom dentures can restore your smile, confidence, and daily comfort. We also provide relines and adjustments to help dentures stay secure over time.

Repair and Replace

Protect the teeth you have and restore the ones you’ve lost

We’ll help you compare options based on durability, appearance, comfort, and long-term oral health.
